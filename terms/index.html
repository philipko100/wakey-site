What Wakey is

Wakey is an alarm app for iPhone. An alarm rings at full system priority and, when you press Stop, books itself again a minute later, until you complete a mission: a math problem, a memory sequence, a phrase to type, a shake, or finding an object with the camera. Some features require the Wakey Pro subscription; which ones may change as the App develops.

Wakey is not a safety device

Whether an alarm rings depends on your iPhone, not on us: the alarm permission you granted, the ringer volume, the Focus settings you chose, the battery, and the version of iOS. Apple's system, not the App, decides whether an alarm sounds. Keep a second alarm for anything you cannot afford to miss. We are not liable for a missed, late or quiet alarm.

The camera mission

"Hunt an object (AI)" sends single camera frames to our server and to a third-party AI provider (OpenAI) to decide whether the asked-for thing is in view; the frames are not stored. The decision can be wrong, which is why a mission can be switched to a math problem after three failed attempts.
