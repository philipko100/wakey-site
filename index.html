Wake up. Finish the mission.

Wakey is an alarm for iPhone that does not end until you finish a mission: a math problem, a memory sequence, a phrase to type, a shake, or finding an object with your camera. Pressing Stop only buys a minute; the alarm comes back until the mission is done.

What it does

  • Rings through Silent and Focus. Built on Apple's AlarmKit, the same system the Clock app uses: a full-screen alarm on the Lock Screen and on your Apple Watch.
  • Missions, not snoozes. Math, memory tiles, typing, shaking, and "Hunt an object (AI)" with the camera. Chain up to five missions on one alarm with Wakey Pro.
  • A safety valve. After three failed attempts a mission can switch to a math problem. The alarm still ends only when a mission is completed.
  • An account, only if you want one. Sign in with Apple or Google to back your alarms up and use them on another iPhone. The app works fully without one.

Wakey is coming to the App Store. See Support for how the alarm works and what to do if it does not ring.
